# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The WooAgent OS community is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone who contributes — across all forms of participation and regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical perspective.

## Standards

We expect contributors to:

- Treat one another with respect, patience, and good faith.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences, and give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when warranted, and learn from the experience.
- Prioritize what is best for the community as a whole over individual preferences.

Behavior that is unacceptable includes:

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments directed at other contributors.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address) without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Adoption

This Code of Conduct adapts and adopts by reference the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Where any term here is ambiguous, the full Contributor Covenant text governs.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull-request reviews, discussions, community channels, and any public spaces where an individual is representing the project — and also applies to behavior outside these spaces when that behavior affects the safety and well-being of community members.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the Automattic Open Source team at <opensource@automattic.com>. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The Automattic Open Source team is obligated to respect the privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident in good faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including temporary or permanent removal from project spaces, in response to behavior that is in violation. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's leadership.
